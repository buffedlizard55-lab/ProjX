Reference profiles — verified without an Instagram or TikTok account.

This page is the other side of the ProjX directory: verified adult (18+) women public figures and professionals who are documented via Wikipedia, personal or agency websites, X, YouTube, Facebook, press, or other trusted public sources — and do not operate a published Instagram or TikTok profile. Every row is reviewed line by line against its stored source links. Profiles that do have an Instagram or TikTok account are on the Instagram / TikTok catalog page instead.

How this page works

  • Each row is a real, adult (18+) woman verified from legitimate public evidence.
  • “Reference” means no public Instagram or TikTok account was found — not that the person is unverified.
  • Follower counts are therefore usually FOLLOWER_COUNT_UNKNOWN (a count is never estimated).
  • Men, couples, group/org accounts, and gender-ambiguous profiles without reliable evidence are excluded.
  • Nothing is inferred from appearance or clothing; categories are objective (Athlete, Creator, Model…).
  • Missing or ambiguous evidence becomes a flagged irregularity, never a guessed catalog row.
