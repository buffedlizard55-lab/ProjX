Verification

Line-by-line checks

Every future row should be independently reviewable from its source links. Records with missing, contradictory, or inferred evidence stay out of the public table and move to the irregularities list.

  1. Confirm legal adult status with official or trusted public sources.
  2. Confirm the profile is controlled by the named person or official representation.
  3. Confirm the person is real and not AI-generated, synthetic, or an impersonation.
  4. Record source URLs, review date, reviewer notes, and unresolved flags.
  5. Publish only records with compliant consent/professional context.
