Every published record is a real adult woman backed by official links.

ProjX is a static, source-backed directory framework for real, adult (18+) women who are public figures and professionals — working models, athletes, creators, and similar public pages — who appear only with professional/public context and official age-and-identity evidence. Each row is reviewed line by line against its stored source links before it is published. No men, couples, group/org accounts, or gender-ambiguous profiles without reliable evidence are accepted, nothing is inferred from appearance alone, and categories are objective (Model, Fitness, Athlete, Fashion, Lifestyle, Creator…), never attractiveness rankings.

Eligibility

What a record must prove

These seven conditions apply to every record regardless of platform or category. Meeting all of them is the only way a row enters the table; anything less goes to the irregularities queue. The database is women-only and categories are objective.

  1. Woman, with evidence. The subject is a woman, confirmed from a reliable public source (first-party bio, official site, agency, sports-org bio, reputable interview) — never from appearance, clothing, name, AI analysis, or username alone. Men, couples, group/org accounts, and gender-ambiguous profiles without reliable evidence are excluded.
  2. Real person. The subject is a real, living individual — not AI, synthetic, a virtual influencer, deepfake, impersonator, or repost account; identity is consistent across official sources.
  3. Adult, with evidence. 18+ is confirmed from an official or trusted public source with a reviewable URL. Age is never inferred from appearance, “college girl” status, school year, content, or follower counts.
  4. Public or professional context. The woman operates a clear public figure/professional page (for example working model, athlete, fitness/fashion/ lifestyle creator, public personality) in an objective category, or has opted in. Private individuals are never inferred into the table.
  5. Official links & objective category. Every record stores at least one source URL marked official, verified-platform, agency, press, or age-evidence (Tier-1 prioritized), so a human reviewer can open it directly; category is Model/Fitness/Athlete/ Fashion/Lifestyle/Creator/Entertainment/College athlete/Public personality/Other, never “hot/sexy” ranking.
  6. Line-by-line review. A reviewer, a review date, and notes are recorded, and each factual field (gender, age, identity, profile, category, sources) is supported by the stored sources; duplicate search precedes any add.
  7. No unresolved flags. Missing, conflicting, or ambiguous evidence produces a flagged irregularity (GENDER_UNVERIFIED, AGE_UNVERIFIED, IDENTITY_UNCERTAIN, POSSIBLE_AI, etc.) — never a guessed value in the table.

Manual review

How a row is checked

Every candidate row is reviewed against its source links before publication. Bulk harvesting cannot satisfy gender, adult-status, and line-by-line checks, and fabricating verification or follower counts would violate the project's no-hallucination rule. Published rows carry reviewable source URLs and, when publicly visible, observed follower counts with checked dates.

The full procedure is defined in the verification protocol; decisions and review notes are tracked in the review log.

  1. Open every stored source URL and confirm it resolves and names the woman.
  2. Confirm the account is operated by the named woman or official representation.
  3. Confirm gender from the reliable public evidence only (not appearance/clothing/name/AI).
  4. Confirm adult status from the official/trusted evidence only (not looks or “college girl”).
  5. Confirm authenticity — no AI, synthetic, virtual-influencer, or impersonation signals.
  6. Confirm professional/public context or opt-in consent and objective category.
  7. De-duplicate by name and all usernames/websites; publish as verified, or record needs-review / blocked with flags.
