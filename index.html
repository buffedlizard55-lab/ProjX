Every published record is real, adult, and backed by official links.

ProjX is a static, source-backed directory framework for real, adult (18+) public figures and professionals — working models, athletes, creators, and similar public pages — who appear only with professional/public context and official age-and-identity evidence. Each row is reviewed line by line against its stored source links before it is published. Nothing is collected from appearance alone, and no private individual is ever inferred into the table.

Eligibility

What a record must prove

These six conditions apply to every record regardless of platform or category. Meeting all of them is the only way a row enters the table; anything less goes to the irregularities queue.

  1. Real person. The subject is a real, living individual — not AI, synthetic, a deepfake, an impersonator, or a repost account.
  2. Adult, with evidence. 18+ is confirmed from an official or trusted public source with a reviewable URL. Age is never inferred from appearance, school year, content, or follower counts.
  3. Public or professional context. The person operates a clear public figure/professional page (for example a working model, athlete, or creator) or has opted in to inclusion.
  4. Official links. Every record stores at least one source URL marked official, verified-platform, agency, press, or age-evidence, so a human reviewer can open it directly.
  5. Line-by-line review. A reviewer, a review date, and notes are recorded, and each factual field is supported by the stored sources.
  6. No unresolved flags. Missing, conflicting, or ambiguous evidence produces a flagged irregularity — never a guessed value in the table.

Manual review

How a row is checked

Every candidate row is reviewed against its source links before publication. The current table is empty because no candidate set has satisfied every check — bulk harvesting cannot satisfy checks 2, 3, and 6 above, and fabricating "verification" would violate the project's no-hallucination rule.

The full procedure is defined in the verification protocol; decisions and review notes are tracked in the review log.

  1. Open every stored source URL and confirm it resolves and names the person.
  2. Confirm the account is operated by the named person or official representation.
  3. Confirm adult status from the official/trusted evidence only.
  4. Confirm authenticity — no AI, synthetic, or impersonation signals.
  5. Confirm professional/public context or opt-in consent.
  6. Publish as verified, or record needs-review / blocked with the reason.
